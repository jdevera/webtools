Giraffedoku 🦒

A giraffe-herding logic puzzle. Place one giraffe in every row, every column and every paddock — but never let two giraffes stand next to each other, not even diagonally, or their necks will tangle. A shared daily puzzle plus endless free play in three difficulties, every board with exactly one solution.

🎉 All necks untangled!
🌳 The herd ate all your trees!

🎉 You did it!

How to play: tap a cell to mark it with ✕ (no giraffe here), tap twice to place or remove a giraffe, and drag across cells to paint or erase ✕ marks. Auto-✕ crosses off everything a placed giraffe rules out. The hint button explains the next logical deduction. You start with three trees: place a giraffe where none belongs and the herd eats one — lose all three and you must start the puzzle over.
