Ispeñul

Translate Spanish into Ispeñul — the word game where every vowel advances one step along a→e→i→o→u→a — and back. Includes the sibling variants Ospiñal (+2), Uspoñel (+3) and Aspuñil (+4).

The rule is phonetic, not typographic: spelling repairs keep every consonant sound intact (carpinteros → querpontirus), stressed diphthongs split with the written accent recomputed (puerta → paírte), and the vocalic y comes and goes (ley → lío, tenue → tínay).
